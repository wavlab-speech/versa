## Evaluate speech, audio, and music

VERSA brings evaluation and profiling metrics into one configurable workflow.
Use reference-free metrics, compare recordings against a reference, connect audio
with text, or compare entire audio collections.

## Start here

1. [Install VERSA](installation/) and the optional dependencies for your metrics.
2. [Run an evaluation](usage/) with the included examples or your own recordings.
3. [Explore the metric catalog](metrics/) to choose what to measure.
4. [Visualize your results](visualization/) with reports, charts, and summaries.

## Choose your metrics

| Category | When to use it |
| --- | --- |
| Independent | Evaluate a recording without reference audio. |
| Dependent | Compare a prediction with its reference recording. |
| Non-match | Use non-matching references or other modalities such as text. |
| Distributional | Compare the statistical properties of audio collections. |

The [metric catalog](metrics/) includes configuration keys, output keys,
dependency information, original implementations, and research references.
Not every metric backend is included in the base installation.
